## Retained identifiers

- `Scroller::unk_0x3C` is the scroll velocity. Its out-of-line `init` in protected `src/scene/sdChannelMemory/iplSDMemory.cpp` writes it, so the ownership rule prevents this rename.
- `ChannelSelect::unkBool` and the pure virtual `AOSSThread::unk_0x2C` retain their function names under the explicit function-renaming prohibition. The latter has no implementation or calls establishing a semantic role.
- `DISK_ANIM_UNKNOWN` and its related names identify the actual Unknown disk animations. They are meaningful asset names, not placeholders.
- `ChannelObj::mbModuleFlag` has only a false constructor initialization. Its name states the module role without claiming unsupported behavior.
